# The second designer long list

Three hundred and twenty more candidate parametric designers were written down
before the second batch was coded. Fifty of them are built on this branch; the
rest stay recorded so the choice is visible rather than implied.

A **✓** marks a designer that shipped in this batch. Together with the
forty-six from `docs/DESIGNERS.md` the catalogue now holds ninety-six designs.

Every candidate was judged on the same four rules as the first list:

1. the geometry follows exactly from a handful of numbers, with a closed-form
   check the app can verify next to the preview;
2. the result is one closed shell that can be made without a fixture in at least one
   orientation;
3. the part is recognisable and useful on its own, not a fragment of a larger
   assembly;
4. it fits an existing kernel here (multi-hole prism, solid of revolution,
   swept tube), so accuracy can be tested rather than asserted.
